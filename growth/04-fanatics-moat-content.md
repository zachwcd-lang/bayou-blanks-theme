# Playbook 4 — The "Fanatics Blanks" Moat

**Goal:** Make "authentic Fanatics blanks at liquidation prices" a story that travels on its own — through TikTok clips, the blog, decorator communities, and industry press. Nobody else can tell this story; it should be everywhere we are.

**The story (canonical version — keep consistent):**
> The same blank apparel used by MLB, NFL, and NHL teams, sourced as authentic Fanatics overstock through licensed liquidation channels. Received, scanned, and quality-checked at our Hattiesburg, MS warehouse. Sold at a fraction of wholesale — live on TikTok daily, no minimums, no contracts.

**KPIs:** branded search ("bayou blanks", "fanatics blanks"), community-referred orders, TikTok→site click-through, newsletter/press mentions.

---

## Phase 1 — Asset kit (week 1) — agent drafts everything

Build once, reuse everywhere. All drafts to `drafts/`:

1. **Wholesale one-pager (PDF):** the DM-able sales asset. Contents: the story, price ladder table ($10/$12/$15 and $16/$19/$34 by pack size), category list with live unit counts, "no account / no minimums / ships from MS", contact + site + TikTok. Agent drafts markdown → produce PDF → Zach approves.
2. **Boilerplate blurbs:** 50-word, 100-word, and 300-word versions of the story for bios, forum signatures, press pitches.
3. **Proof pack:** price-comparison table (our price vs S&S Activewear/SanMar list prices for comparable blanks — research current prices via web), unit-count screenshots, review quotes from TikTok/Whatnot.
4. **Clip brief for the stream team:** 5 recurring TikTok clip formats —
   - "Pallet cracking" (opening sealed Fanatics case packs — provenance porn)
   - "Price math" (this hoodie is $24 wholesale anywhere else, $10 here, here's why)
   - "Print test" (a local printer prints on our blank — UGC partnership)
   - "Warehouse tour / day in the life, Hattiesburg"
   - "What's left" (scarcity inventory rundown → drives email signups, Playbook 1)

## Phase 2 — Community seeding (weeks 1–4, then ongoing)

Rules: be useful first, disclose affiliation always, never spam. One community at a time.

1. **Map the watering holes** (agent, via web search): r/screenprinting, r/printondemand, r/Whatnot, Facebook groups (screen printing, embroidery, apparel resellers), t-shirt forums, Discords. Log each with rules on self-promo → `drafts/community-map.md`.
2. **Per-community plan:** read the room for a week → answer questions helpfully → then one disclosure-forward intro post ("We liquidate authentic Fanatics blanks from a Mississippi warehouse — AMA / here's our price sheet"). Agent drafts each post; Zach (or a team member) posts from a human account — platforms punish brand-account spam.
3. **Local angle:** MS/LA/AL print shops — agent builds a list (web search: screen printers within ~150mi of Hattiesburg) with contact info → `drafts/local-printers.md`. Outreach email/DM draft offering a sample case at cost + the one-pager. Goal: 2–3 shops as repeat wholesale accounts and UGC partners.

## Phase 3 — Press & newsletters (weeks 2–6)

1. Targets (agent researches current editors/contacts): Impressions Magazine, Printwear, Screen Printing Mag, PRINTING United, t-shirt/POD newsletters, liquidation/reseller YouTubers and podcasters.
2. Pitch angle: "TikTok liquidator is selling the same blanks pro teams wear for $10" — it's a story, not an ad. Draft pitch email + the proof pack.
3. Whatnot/TikTok Shop themselves feature notable sellers — apply/pitch for seller spotlights (agent researches current programs).

## Phase 4 — On-site reinforcement (with Playbook 3)

- About page = canonical story (Playbook 3 owns execution).
- Blog post: "What are Fanatics blanks?" — aims to own that search term outright.
- Homepage already carries the trust blocks; keep story wording synced with the canonical version above.
